Curio: your knowledge, out to play

A personal second-brain studio where your Google Docs become artifacts you can drag onto a whiteboard, fork into fresh LLM threads, deal into slide decks, flip as flashcards, quiz against YouTube videos, and listen to out loud. Docs are where your thinking sleeps — Curio is where it wakes up.

THE BIG PICTURE

One library, many playgrounds

The center of gravity is your artifact library — every Google Doc, note, and flashcard becomes a small, colorful, recallable object. Every experience around the edge is just a different way to play with the same objects. Nothing is siloed: a flashcard born in a YouTube quiz can land on the board, join a deck, and get read aloud on a walk.

why this shape: you never "open the knowledge base app." you open whichever mood you're in — build, drill, watch, listen — and the same artifacts follow you there.

DEMO 01

The Board — thinking with your hands

An Excalidraw-style canvas that is aware of your library. Stickies for raw thought, and real doc artifacts you pin from the shelf — each one a live card that remembers where it came from. Scenario below: you're building your interview-prep framework.

DEMO 02

Thread forking — take your context, leave the thread whole

The LLM habit you described, made first-class: pick the messages that matter, fork them into a fresh thread. The original conversation stays intact and readable. Context travels as references, so forks are instant and the family tree stays visible.

→ check the two messages about your migration story, then fork

💬 interview-prep original

you
Help me build a story bank for behavioral interviews.
AI
Start with 5 stories that each flex a different muscle: leadership, conflict, failure, ambiguity, impact…
you
Here's my Q3 migration story — 40% infra cost down, two teams unblocked. Tighten it?
AI
Tightened: lead with the stakes, then the 40%, then what you'd do differently. Numbers first, adjectives second.
the fork will appear here —
your original thread won't even notice

in prod: threads store a context ledger — a list of message and artifact references, not copies. Forking copies the ledger and a parent pointer. Cheap, instant, and the tree view falls out for free.

DEMO 03

Instant decks — teach yourself in 30 seconds

Pick artifacts, get a deck. Not a design tool — a self-teaching tool. The LLM turns each artifact into a slide with the two or three things worth saying out loud, in your sticky-note visual language, ready to present to an audience of one: you.

→ check a few docs, then build the deck. arrows flip slides
your deck shows up here — pick some docs above ☝︎

in prod: deck generation is a one-shot LLM pipeline (artifacts → outline → slide JSON → your visual theme). Decks are artifacts too, so they land back in the library — and can be narrated by the audio engine below.

DEMO 04

The Constellation — flashcards that know each other

Flashcards aren't a stack, they're nodes in your knowledge graph. Edges come from your docs, your board links, and embedding similarity. Drilling a card lights up its neighborhood, so review stops being rote and starts being a little walk through connected ideas.

→ tap a star to flip its card; "got it" marks your streak

pick a node from the constellation ✦

in prod: spaced repetition (FSRS) picks tonight's cards, but review happens in graph context — neighbors shown, edges explained. Cards are born everywhere: highlight on the board, answer a video question, ask the LLM "make this a card."

DEMO 06

Read It To Me — your notes, in your ears

Any artifact — a note, a doc summary, a deck, tonight's flashcards — becomes an audio snippet. For the audio-learner half of your brain: queue up your own thinking like a podcast.

→ edit the note, then hit listen (this demo uses your browser's voice)

in prod: ElevenLabs voices (pick one you love, keep it consistent — it becomes "the voice of your brain"), generated server-side and cached as MP3s, with a daily auto-episode: "your 5 cards due + yesterday's board, 4 minutes."

BUILD ORDER

Four slices, each fun on its own

Each slice is a complete toy — you get a real daily-use win before the next one starts. No slice depends on a future slice to be worth having.

① The Board + Shelf

  • Excalidraw embed + artifact side panel
  • Google Drive sync (read-only first)
  • Pin docs, stickies, manual links
the day-one habit: think here, not in a doc

weeks 4–6
  • LLM deck pipeline + sticky theme
  • Context ledger + fork UI
  • Decks saved back as artifacts

③ Constellation

weeks 7–9
  • Card creation from any surface
  • FSRS scheduling + graph edges
  • Nightly review walk
retention becomes a game you keep

④ Watch + Listen

  • YouTube queue + checkpoint quizzes
  • ElevenLabs pipeline + daily episode

learning follows you off the screen
